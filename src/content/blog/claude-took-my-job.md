Fifteen years in: five in healthcare software, eight consulting on enterprise CMS, five (and
counting) on Xbox Player Experiences. I wrote code, reviewed code, shipped code. That was the job.

Then Claude started writing a lot of the code — faster, tirelessly, and often well enough that
competing with it stopped being the point. The useful reframe wasn't "AI replaced me," it was
"I have a very capable, very junior engineer who needs direction, review, and guardrails." That's
a management problem, not an existential one.

So this site is the record of that shift: the tooling I build to make an AI collaborator
trustworthy, reviewable, and accountable — [CMUX Maestro](/projects/cmux-maestro) for visibility,
[PR Sniper](/projects/pr-sniper) for human-owned review gates, [Cacophony](/projects/cacophony) for
adversarial review from the AI side, and [dotfiles](/projects/dotfiles) for a reproducible
environment underneath all of it.

More to come as the backlog burns down — my direct report doesn't take PTO.
